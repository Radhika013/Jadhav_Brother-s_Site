About us

Building products. Building brands.

Jadhav Brother's Telient Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian company focused on providing OEM and ODM solutions for electrical and electronic products.

We work with businesses, distributors, retailers and entrepreneurs who want to develop, manufacture and launch products under their own brand. Our objective is to make product development and private-label manufacturing more accessible by bringing together product sourcing, development, manufacturing coordination, quality management and branding support.
